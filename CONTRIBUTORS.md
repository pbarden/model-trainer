- Documentation updates

### Research Contributions

### Community Contributions

## Recognition

Contributors will be recognized in the following ways:

1. **Name added to this file** - All meaningful contributions are acknowledged
2. **GitHub contribution graph** - Commits and PRs are tracked automatically
3. **Release notes** - Significant contributions mentioned in CHANGELOG.md
4. **Special recognition** - Outstanding contributions may receive additional acknowledgment
